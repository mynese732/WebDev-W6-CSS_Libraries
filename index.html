About these games

Hollow Knight and Hollow Knight: Silksong

Hollow Knight and it's sequel, Hollow Knight: Silksong, are 2d metroidvania games that are known for their beautiful art style, challenging gameplay, and amazing music. The games take place in fallen kingdoms that the player must explore and uncover the secrets within. Due to their difficulty, these games are not for everyone, even earning the label "souls like" in reference to the difficult Dark Souls series created by FromSoftware. I fell in love with the first game in high school and the second game years later when it came out in 2025. I have struggled and succeeded in getting even the most difficult achievements in both games.
