About these games (cont'd):

Destiny 2

Destiny 2 is a multiplayer science fiction first-person shooter video game developed by Bungie. The game takes place in a shared universe where players can engage in various activities such as PvE missions, PvP matches, and cooperative gameplay. The original Destiny game was one of my first main games growing up and the second, while not perfect, introduced a lot of quality of life improvements and quality story/content updates. This game is the one with my most playing hours on.

Terraria

Terraria is a 2D sandbox adventure game developed by Re-Logic. The game allows players to explore a randomly generated world, mine resources, craft items, and defeat enemies. It features a variety bosses and types of biomes, each with unique characteristics and challenges. I have played Terraria many times over the years, often with my brother and have formed a lot of good memories playing it. Additionally, the developers have continued to support the game with a lot of updates and new content, keeping the experience fresh and engaging for players.

Minecraft

Minecraft, much like Terraria, is a sandbox game that allows players to explore and create in a blocky, procedurally generated world. The game offers various modes, including survival, creative, and adventure, each with its own set of challenges and objectives. This game has been one of the main games I have played with friends.
